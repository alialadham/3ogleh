About 3ogleh

Jordanian-made calisthenics equipment built for serious training.

3ogleh | عقلة is a Jordanian fitness brand built for calisthenics athletes who demand more from their equipment. We create durable, high-performance gear designed to withstand intense training sessions while maintaining a clean, athletic look.

Founded in Jordan, our mission is simple: build equipment that holds weight, outlasts training, and helps athletes perform at their best. We believe every athlete is unique, which is why we offer customizable calisthenics gear tailored to individual needs and training styles.

At 3ogleh, we combine strength, functionality, and design to support athletes on their journey toward mastering bodyweight fitness.

Mission

At 3ogleh, our mission is to provide calisthenics athletes with high-quality, durable, and customizable training equipment that enhances performance, supports progression, and withstands the demands of intense training.

Vision

To become a globally recognized calisthenics brand, empowering athletes around the world with innovative, durable, and customizable training equipment that inspires strength, performance, and growth.

Brand Values

Durable manufacturing Clean design Custom fit Athlete-first functionality Pride in Jordanian calisthenics culture
